## Description

BirthdayBuddy is your personal celebration assistant.

Remember birthdays, anniversaries, and special days with smart reminders. Import contacts, complete profiles, and open anyone's details from your calendar.

Create AI-powered wishes, design cards, and build surprise links for unforgettable moments.

**Premium** adds unlimited AI wishes, premium templates, HD exports, and priority features. Invite friends with Refer & Earn to unlock Premium rewards.

Privacy-first: your data stays on your device with offline SQLite storage.
